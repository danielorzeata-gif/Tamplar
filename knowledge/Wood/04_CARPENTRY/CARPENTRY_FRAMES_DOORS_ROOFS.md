# CARPENTRY — frames, doors, roofs, timber structures, structural connections

Surse: Fairham `[V-TEXT]` (usi, sarpante, scarf); Edwin W. Foster, *Carpentry and Woodwork* (Gutenberg #43574) `[REF]` (descarcat, neextras complet); I.S. Griffith, *Carpentry* (Gutenberg #70226) `[REF]`; Shawver, *Plank Frame Barn Construction* (Gutenberg #65399) `[REF]`; Tedd Benson, *Building the Timber Frame House* (1980) `[REF]`; Jack Sobon, *Build a Classic Timber-Framed House* (1994) `[REF]`; Will Beemer, *Learn to Timber Frame* (2016) `[REF]`; EN 1995-1-1 `[V-STD]`; Klaus Zwerger, *Wood and Wood Joints* (Birkhäuser, 1997/2012) `[REF]`.

## Usi (doors)
**CARP-DR-001** — Usa cu panouri: montanti + traverse (sus, mijloc/"lock rail", jos) cu M&T haunched (haunch = canalul panoului); horn la montanti; traverse +12 mm cu cep trecut care iese ~6 mm; pene la cepurile trecute. SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 130–132, 176–177 `[V-TEXT]`.
**CARP-DR-002** — Usa de lambriu (ledged & braced): scanduri T&G pe traverse + contrafise diagonale **care urca spre partea balamalelor** (lucreaza in compresiune). SOURCE: `[PRACTICA]` (universal in literatura de dulgherie; Foster `[REF]`).
**CARP-DR-003** — Imbinarea tenon cu umeri faltuiti (long-and-short shoulder) la tocuri si cadre faltuite. SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 143 `[V-TEXT]`.
**CARP-DR-004** — Usi exterioare: lemn stabil (stejar, larice, meranti, lemn lamelat 3 straturi), adeziv D4, prag cu lacrimar, finisaj pigmentat. SOURCE: `[PRACTICA]`; EN 942 `[V-STD]`.

## Sarpante si structuri (roofs / timber structures)
**CARP-RF-001** — Ferma cu popi (king post truss): capriorii principali cu cep in pop; contrafise cu cep in pop; pop cu cep in tirant (tie beam). SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 160–161 `[V-TEXT]`.
**CARP-RF-002** — Cep tusk cu pana pentru grinda secundara in grinda principala; scobitura in zona comprimata, langa axa neutra. SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 153 `[V-TEXT]`.
**CARP-RF-003** — Inadirea panelor: scarf ≈ 4 × inaltimea, cu doua chei de esenta tare; pozitionat langa reazem. SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 218 `[V-TEXT]`.
**CARP-RF-004** — Structurile moderne (case, sarpante) se dimensioneaza dupa EC5 + anexa nationala + CR 0 / P100 (Romania, seism) si se folosesc conectori metalici certificati (ETA). Imbinarile traditionale lemn-lemn sunt admise dar trebuie verificate. SOURCE: `[V-STD]`. (Pentru proiecte de case vezi si skill-ul local `casa-de-lemn`.)
**CARP-TF-001 — Timber framing traditional european**: stalpi + grinzi + contravantuiri cu M&T si cuie de lemn (drawbore), scarf pentru prelungiri; trasare "scribe rule" (fiecare imbinare potrivita individual) vs "square rule" (cote teoretice in interiorul lemnului — permite prefabricare). SOURCE: Benson; Sobon; Beemer `[REF]`.
**CARP-TF-002 — Triangulare vs ductilitate**: traditia europeana rigidizeaza cu diagonale (triunghi); traditia japoneza cu nuki + pene (ductil). SOURCE: Zwerger; Coaldrake `[REF]`.

## Conexiuni structurale (structural connections)
| Tip | Utilizare | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lemn–lemn traditional (M&T, scarf, dovetail) | timber frame, restaurare | verificare EC5 la compresiune perpendiculara, forfecare cep, cui de lemn |
| Cuie, suruburi, buloane, dibluri metalice | structuri usoare/grele | EC5 cap. 8: distante minime (a1–a4), pre-gaurire pentru esente tari si diametre mari |
| Placi multi-cui (gang nail) | ferme prefabricate | proiectare de producator |
| Suruburi autoforante lungi | CLT, armare | ETA |
| Sabot metalic (joist hanger) | grinzi in grinzi | rapid |

**CARP-CON-001** — Pre-gaurire obligatorie in esente tari (stejar, salcam) pentru cuie/suruburi — altfel despicare. SOURCE: EN 1995-1-1 §8 `[V-STD]`; `[PRACTICA]`.
