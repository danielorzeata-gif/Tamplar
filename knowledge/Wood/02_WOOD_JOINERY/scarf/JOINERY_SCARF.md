**JOINERY-SC-001** — Scarf de pana: lungime ≈ 4 × inaltimea grinzii, doua chei de esenta tare. SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 218 `[V-TEXT]`.
**JOINERY-SC-002** — Tabled scarf: lungime ≈ 5 × grosimea; buna la tractiune si compresiune; afectata putin de contractie. SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 219 `[V-TEXT]`.
**JOINERY-SC-003** — Fished joint ≠ scarf: eclise laterale, piesele cap la cap; cheile de lemn previn "creeping". SOURCE: Fairham Fig. 217 `[V-TEXT]`.
**JOINERY-SC-004** — Pozitioneaza inadirea langa reazem (moment incovoietor mic). SOURCE: `[PRACTICA]`; Eurocod 5 pentru verificare `[V-STD]`.
