# TRADITIONAL WOODWORKING (european) — tehnici si principii

Surse: Fairham `[V-TEXT]`; Noyes 1910 `[V-TEXT]` (partial); Zwerger `[REF]`; Welsh *Woodworking Tools 1600–1900* `[REF]`; Mike Abbott, *Green Woodwork* `[REF]`; Drew Langsner, *Green Woodworking* `[REF]`.

**TRAD-001 — Originea mobilierului englez in lada din stejar** (constructie din rama si panou sau scanduri groase cu cepuri). SOURCE: Fairham, frontispiciu "Old Oak Chests, showing the Method of Structure which forms the origin of most of our English Furniture" `[V-TEXT]`.
**TRAD-002 — Drawbore** — fixare fara clema si fara adeziv (Fairham Fig. 162) — permite structuri durabile la exterior. `[V-TEXT]`
**TRAD-003 — Clei animal** — adezivul traditional (reversibil); Fairham: consistenta smantanii, fierbinte, lemn incalzit. `[V-TEXT]`
**TRAD-004 — Imbinari exterioare istorice cu vopsea/miniu de plumb in loc de clei** (Fairham) — azi interzis/inlocuit (toxic). `[V-TEXT]`
**TRAD-005 — Lemn verde despicat (riven / green woodworking)**: piese despicate urmeaza fibra → rezistenta maxima (scaune Windsor, unelte); cepuri din lemn uscat in scobituri din lemn verde → se strang la uscare. SOURCE: Abbott; Langsner `[REF]`.
**TRAD-006 — Lemnul curb natural** folosit pentru piese curbe (genunchi de nava, cruck frames) — fibra continua. SOURCE: Zwerger `[REF]`.
**TRAD-007 — Tusk tenon, pene si chei** — mobilier si structuri demontabile (Fairham Fig. 152–157). `[V-TEXT]`
**TRAD-008 — Imbinari "puzzle"** (Fairham cap. Puzzle Joints) — exercitii de trasare 3D; util pentru instruire. `[V-TEXT]`
**TRAD-009 — Traditii romanesti** (iteratia 5): constructia din barne (Blockbau, "cununi orizontale"), cheotori (rotunde/ciobanesti, "nemtesti"/trapezoidale in coada de randunica, "cheia batraneasca"), talpi, cosoroabe, cuie de lemn, specii pe zone, perioada de taiere, degradare (ciuperci, insecte), mobilier vernacular fara adeziv. Fisa completa, cu 7 surse legale citite: `02_WOOD_JOINERY/traditional_joinery/JOINERY_ROMANIAN_LOG_CONSTRUCTION.md`.
