# Assembly sequence

1. Dry-fit all parts without glue and check squareness.
2. Glue up Table top (edge-glued) (see part operations).
3. Glue and clamp joint J001: APR-L-1 -> LEG-1 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
4. Glue and clamp joint J002: APR-L-1 -> LEG-2 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
5. Glue and clamp joint J003: APR-L-2 -> LEG-3 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
6. Glue and clamp joint J004: APR-L-2 -> LEG-4 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
7. Glue and clamp joint J005: APR-S-1 -> LEG-1 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
8. Glue and clamp joint J006: APR-S-1 -> LEG-3 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
9. Glue and clamp joint J007: APR-S-2 -> LEG-2 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
10. Glue and clamp joint J008: APR-S-2 -> LEG-4 (Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm)
11. Install 10 x TOP-ZCLIP (see hardware installation details).
12. Install 2 x TOP-SLOTSCREW (see hardware installation details).
13. Finish (oil/lacquer) and cure; fit top with fasteners last.
