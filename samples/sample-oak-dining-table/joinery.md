# Joinery details

## J001 - Mortise & Tenon (APR-L-1 -> LEG-1)
Mortise & Tenon: tenon 8x60x40 mm

_Tenon thickness = 1/3 of rail thickness, tenon length = 1/2 of the receiving member (capped), shoulders on the rail edges. Mortise is cut slightly deeper than the tenon to leave a glue pocket. Where two mortises meet in a corner leg the tenon ends are mitred._

- tenonThickness: 8
- tenonLength: 40
- tenonWidth: 60
- mortiseDepth: 43
- feature on LEG-1: Mortise 60.4x43x8.4 (tool MORT-8)
- feature on APR-L-1: Tenon 40x60x8 (tool SAW-TS-300)
